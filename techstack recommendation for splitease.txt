Why this wins on your three constraints

- Maintain: nothing to patch or keep alive — no Node process, no Docker image, no better-sqlite3, no cron of backups. This is the actual lever for "easy to maintain," more than any hosting choice.
- Cost: at 30-40 users, you don't leave the free tier — Supabase free tier covers 500MB DB, 50k monthly active users, 1GB file storage, 500k edge-function calls; Cloudflare Pages is free for the frontend. Supabase pricing 2026
- Reliable: Supabase runs backups/updates/failover for you (paid tier gets you SLA/backups if you ever need it — still cheap at $25/mo, and splitting that 30-40 ways is a joke your own app could settle).
